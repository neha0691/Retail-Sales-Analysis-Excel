# Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales transaction data to identify sales patterns, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, and key revenue drivers.The analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel, following an end-to-end data analytics workflow including data quality checks, feature engineering, exploratory data analysis, KPI development, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and dashboard design.
The final outcome is a Retail Sales Performance Dashboard that provides a concise overview of sales performance and highlights key business insights.

# Project Objectives
The main objectives of this project were to:

- Clean and validate the retail sales dataset.
- Check the dataset for missing values, duplicates, inconsistencies, and outliers.
- Create new analytical features such as Age Group, Month, Quarter, and Day Name.
- Analyse sales performance across product categories.
- Identify monthly sales trends.
- Compare revenue generated by gender and customer age groups.
- Analyse product quantity and average selling price.
- Calculate key business KPIs.
- Build a professional Excel dashboard to communicate business insights.

# Business Questions
The analysis was designed to answer the following business questions:

Which product category generates the highest revenue?
Which month records the highest sales?
Which gender generates higher revenue?
Which customer age group contributes the most revenue?
Which product category sells the highest quantity?
Which product category has the highest average selling price?
What is the average order value?
These questions were selected to understand both overall sales performance and customer purchasing behaviour.

# Key Business Insights
The analysis produced the following key findings:

- Electronics generated the highest revenue among the three product categories.
- May recorded the highest monthly sales during the analysed period.
- Female customers generated slightly higher revenue than male customers.
- Middle-aged customers contributed the highest revenue among the age groups.
- Clothing had the highest quantity sold, indicating strong sales volume.
- Beauty products had the highest average selling price among the product categories.
- The overall average order value was $456.
These findings can help a business understand which products, customer segments, and periods contribute most to its sales performance.
